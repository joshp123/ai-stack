# Clear public writing

Write for a cold reader. Put the decision, action or key fact first. Use plain, precise words, active verbs and concrete details. Keep technical substance and necessary nuance; remove only wording that makes it harder to understand.

Keep code, commands, paths, logs, identifiers, quotations, legal text and source material exact.

Before you finish, check that a reader unfamiliar with the work can understand what matters, why it matters and what to do next on the first read. Cut anything that does not help them.
